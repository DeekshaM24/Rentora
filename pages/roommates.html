How Preference Match is calculated

We compare 11 lifestyle dimensions. For sleep schedule and cleanliness, differing selections earn 35% and 50% of that category's weight; a shared value or a flexible selection earns full weight. Other different lifestyle selections earn no weight. Location earns full weight when city or neighborhood text overlaps. Budget is the lower budget divided by the higher budget. Move-in dates earn full weight within 7 days, 75% within 30 days, 50% within 60 days, and no weight beyond that.

  • Location: 20%
  • Budget: 20%
  • Move-in timing: 10%
  • Sleep schedule, cleanliness, smoking, and pets: 8% each
  • Food preference and work / study schedule: 5% each
  • Social preference and noise tolerance: 4% each

Age, occupation, and gender preference are not scored. The result is a preference comparison only; talk directly and make your own decision.
